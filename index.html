Shirin Dehghani

I am currently an AI Engineer at Snapp!Shop, an Iranian e-commerce company under the Snapp Group. Previously, I worked as a Data Scientist at Metodata, where I specialized in computational social sciences. My work focuses on fine-tuning open- and closed-source Large Language Models for diverse applications, optimizing advanced prompts, and developing AI-driven solutions — including LLM-based agents and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems. These solutions have been used for tasks such as data enrichment, conversational AI, and recommender bots.

I hold a Master’s degree in Artificial Intelligence from Allameh Tabataba’i University, where my thesis explored the use of LLM embeddings for Persian stance detection and the development of a Persian benchmark, under the guidance of Dr. Mohammad Reza A. Oskoei.

My research interests span Natural Language Processing (NLP), Large Language Models (LLM), and Reinforcement Learning in LLM. I am particularly interested in issues of reliability and bias in AI, as well as the application of AI technologies in domains such as social sciences, healthcare, and finance.

I have also collaborated with the University of Padua on developing an innovative matrix completion method to enhance recommender systems, and I’m passionate about the ethical implications of datasets and AI technologies.
