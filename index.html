Sherin Dehghani

AI Software Engineer · LLMs & AI Agents

I am an AI Software Engineer at Snapp!Shop, where I design and ship large language model and AI-agent systems for conversational AI, data enrichment, and recommender systems. Previously, I worked as a Data Scientist at Metodata.

I am also a Graduate Research Assistant at Allameh Tabataba'i University, working at the intersection of large language models and computational social science — including fact-checking, text annotation, and stance detection in Persian.

My interests span LLMs, AI agents, NLP, reinforcement learning, and computational economics, with a focus on building reliable, useful, and ethically grounded AI systems.
