Shirin Dehghani

I am an AI Engineer at Snapp!Shop, with prior experience as a Data Scientist at Metodata. My expertise includes fine-tuning large language models, prompt engineering, and developing AI-driven solutions for data enrichment, conversational AI, and recommender systems.

I hold a Master’s in Artificial Intelligence from Allameh Tabataba’i University, where I researched LLM embeddings for Persian stance detection under Dr. Mohammad Reza A. Oskoei.

My research interests include NLP, LLMs, reinforcement learning, and human-AI interaction, with a focus on reliability, bias, and ethical AI in social sciences, healthcare, and finance.

I have collaborated with the University of Padua on recommender systems and am passionate about the ethical implications of AI technologies.
