Shirin Dehghani

I am a graduate research assistant and data scientist with a deep passion for advancing technology in Natural Language Processing (NLP), Large Language Models (LLMs), and Artificial Intelligence (AI).

I recently earned my Master’s degree in Artificial Intelligence from Allameh Tabataba’i University. My thesis focused on leveraging LLM embeddings for Persian stance detection and developing a Persian benchmark, under the supervision of Dr. Mohammad Reza A. Oskoei.

I currently work as a Data Scientist at Metodata, a company specializing in computational social sciences. My role involves fine-tuning large language models (LLMs) such as LLaMA and GPT-3.5 for diverse tasks, optimizing advanced prompts, and developing AI-driven applications, including LLM-based agents and other innovative solutions.

Additionally, I have collaborated with the University of Padova on a research project where I developed an innovative matrix completion method to enhance recommender systems.
