Blog
Natural Language Processing (NLP)

NLP is at the core of my research and work. I am fascinated by how machines can understand, interpret, and generate human language, and I enjoy exploring new models and applications in this field.

Large Language Models (LLMs)

I am passionate about fine-tuning and deploying LLMs for real-world applications, from conversational agents to data enrichment. I am also interested in the challenges of reliability, bias, and ethical use of these models.

Reinforcement Learning in LLM

My work often involves using reinforcement learning to improve LLMs, making them more adaptive and capable in complex tasks. I am particularly interested in how RL can help align models with human values and goals.

Human-AI & Human-Computer Interaction

I believe that the future of AI lies in seamless collaboration between humans and machines. My interests include designing interfaces and systems that foster effective, ethical, and enjoyable human-AI interaction.

Ethics & Societal Impact

I am deeply concerned with the ethical implications of AI and data-driven technologies, especially in sensitive domains like healthcare, finance, and social sciences. I advocate for responsible AI development and transparent, fair datasets.
